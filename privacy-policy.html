On Track — Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 28, 2026

The short version

On Track does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything stays on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no ads.

What data the app stores

On Track stores the following data locally on your device using your browser's localStorage (or the app's internal storage on Android):

Your country selection, habit names and completion status, daily mood logs, journal entries, challenge progress and streak data, notification preferences, and theme settings.

Where data is stored

All data is stored exclusively on your device. Nothing is sent to any server, cloud service, or third party. The app makes zero network requests. It works entirely offline.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect any personal information, usage data, analytics, crash reports, or telemetry of any kind.

Third-party services

On Track does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

Accounts and authentication

On Track does not require or support user accounts. There is no login, no registration, and no authentication.

Children's privacy

On Track does not collect data from anyone, including children. The app contains no age-restricted content.

Deleting your data

You can delete all of your data at any time by opening Settings within the app and tapping "Reset everything." This permanently erases all stored data from your device. You can also uninstall the app, which removes all associated data.

Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL. Since the app stores no data externally, any policy change would only reflect changes to the app itself.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us at: ontrackapp@proton.me
